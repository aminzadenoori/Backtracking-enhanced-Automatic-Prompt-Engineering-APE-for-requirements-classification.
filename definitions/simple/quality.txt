Quality: requirements expressing how well a system or service should execute an intended function, addressing product-quality aspects (functional suitability, reliability, performance, efficiency, usability, maintainability, security, compatibility, portability) and quality-in-use aspects (satisfaction, effectiveness, freedom from risk, efficiency, context coverage). They concern global properties of the system rather than specific functions.
Non-quality: requirements that do not express how well a system executes a function and that do not address product-quality or quality-in-use aspects; instead they concern the properties of specific functions.
